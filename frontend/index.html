We believe financial records belong exclusively to the merchant. Ledger records, customer names, debit/credit tallies, and voice transcripts are preserved directly in your browser's encrypted local client storage.

Microphone access is requested strictly upon user initiation via the "Voice Chat" button. The live speech stream is converted in real time to text for extracting contact and numeric values, then immediately cleared from transient memory.
