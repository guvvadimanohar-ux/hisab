Cookies and storage policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-05

Essential cookies

hisab_session_id is a first-party, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie used to authenticate your account. It expires after seven days. It is essential for sign-in, not an advertising cookie. Logging out revokes the server session and clears the browser cookie.

Device storage

Local storage holds your offline ledger, saved shop profile, display language, consent preferences and the acknowledgement of the storage notice. The service worker caches application assets for offline use. These records can remain until you remove them; they are not protected by application-level encryption.

Optional tracking

Google Analytics 4 is optional and blocked until you allow analytics. When configured it can set _ga and _ga_* cookies for public-page visit measurement. Advertising features are disabled. The business dashboard is excluded. Necessary only declines analytics; withdraw permission using Storage preferences or Settings → Privacy and permissions. Withdrawal removes Analytics cookies on this site and reloads an active public page to stop its tag. The cookie banner offers Necessary only or optional local campaign tags. If you opt in, only utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term and utm_content from the page URL are saved on this device, with the time recorded. No campaign tags are stored before opt-in or sent to another service. Change or withdraw this choice in Settings → Privacy and permissions; withdrawal clears stored tags.

Manage storage

Use Privacy and permissions in Settings to review AI/voice permissions or reopen the storage notice. Browser settings can block or erase cookies and storage. Blocking the session cookie prevents sign-in. Clearing site storage deletes offline records and preferences: export first. It does not delete server records.

External services

AI providers receive requests only when you use the optional assistant after consent. Browser speech services may handle audio after you choose voice. External sites such as WhatsApp and a bank consent page manage their own cookies after you open them.

Operator and privacy contact

Operator legal name, business address and privacy contact are pending configuration. The public service is not ready to launch until these details are verified.
