Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-05

Who is responsible

Hisab is a bookkeeping application. The operator of this deployment is responsible for your account data; each shop is responsible for the customer records it enters. Operator and privacy contact details are shown below. Do not send passwords, Aadhaar numbers, card details or bank credentials to the assistant.

Data and purposes

To create an account we need a shop name, owner name, password and one contact identifier. Email is optional when a phone number is supplied. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Optional shop profile details are used to display and manage your business. Customer names, optional contact details, ledger entries, balances and uploaded statements are used for bookkeeping and summaries. Please enter only information necessary for your records and obtain an appropriate basis and notice for your customers.

Where records are stored

Offline data is stored on your device, without application-level encryption. Signed-in data is stored on the configured server. Essential session cookies keep you signed in; local storage holds offline records and preferences. Protect shared devices and export a backup before clearing data.

Optional AI and voice

AI is optional. After your consent, your question and relevant business context or verified metrics can be sent to the configured AI provider (currently Groq on this deployment); the provider may process data outside India under its own terms. Customer names or amounts included in your request can be sent. AI commands can create ledger entries: review the result. Voice starts only after you choose it and grant microphone permission. Browser speech recognition may send audio to your browser vendor, even when the ledger is offline. Vendor retention is outside this app’s control. Typing and manual entry remain available without voice or AI consent.

Other providers and tracking

Bank linking and payments use the provider displayed at the time of the request and require their own consent or authorisation. Google Analytics 4 may load on public website pages only after you explicitly allow analytics. It receives page-view information and technical/device data and may process data outside India. The dashboard, customer records, transactions and AI conversations are excluded. Advertising features are disabled. Withdraw permission using Storage preferences or Settings → Privacy and permissions. No external fonts or third-party embeds are loaded. Optional local campaign attribution stores UTM tags only after you opt in and can be withdrawn in Settings. WhatsApp reminder links open WhatsApp only when you choose to send a reminder. The server may retain operational security logs; these are not advertising analytics.

Retention and erasure

Active records remain until you remove them or delete your account. Clear Transactions and Clear Customers keep an undo copy and are not permanent erasure. Account deletion removes your account and associated data from the active application database, including undo copies and AI action receipts. It cannot erase exports, external-provider records, security logs or operator backups. The operator must publish its backup and security-log retention schedule before public launch; no schedule has been configured yet.

Your choices and rights

In Settings → Privacy and permissions you can export your account data, correct your profile, withdraw optional AI or voice consent and permanently delete your account after password verification. You may ask the operator about access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal or nomination where applicable under Indian data-protection law. Customers whose data is entered by a shop should contact that shop first. Bank consent is withdrawn through the bank connection/provider controls.

Children and changes

Account registration is for people aged 18 or older who are authorised to manage the shop. Do not enter children’s personal data in this version: a verified parental-consent flow has not been implemented. Updated notices carry a new effective date. This notice describes the implemented features; it is not a certification of legal compliance.

Operator and privacy contact

Operator legal name, business address and privacy contact are pending configuration. The public service is not ready to launch until these details are verified.
