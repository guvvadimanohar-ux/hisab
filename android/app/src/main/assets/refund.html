Refund and cancellation policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-05

Current service

This deployment currently has no paid Hisab subscription or checkout. There is no Hisab subscription charge to cancel or refund. We do not advertise a refund guarantee or invent a refund deadline for a service that is not sold.

Shop and provider payments

Payments recorded in a shop ledger, customer loan repayments and payments handled by a linked bank or payment gateway are separate from Hisab fees. Contact the receiving shop or payment provider for their cancellation, dispute and refund rules. Hisab cannot reverse a settled payment simply by deleting a ledger entry.

If billing is introduced

Before taking payment, the operator must publish the paid plan, complete price and taxes, renewal/cancellation steps, applicable refund conditions and processing times. Mandatory consumer rights continue to apply. Do not collect subscription payments under this notice.

Support

For an unexpected Hisab charge, contact the operator using the verified details below and provide a transaction reference and date. Never send a full card number, PIN, OTP or account password.

Operator and privacy contact

Operator legal name, business address and privacy contact are pending configuration. The public service is not ready to launch until these details are verified.
